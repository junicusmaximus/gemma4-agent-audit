## Why
The train/serve grid is complete and the episode-level collapse persists in every cell (sealed M0, M0q v054, M0 matched v051, M0q matched v055: resolution 0/120 each; loop 0.62-0.79; submit 0.45-0.61; think 0). Hq1 measures the total effect of a history-preserving packing with everything else held at the M0q recipe. It is Codex's "Hq1" and the H1 packing rule of the proposal (sha 6906fbbd) applied on the M0q base; the name change bypasses nothing.
