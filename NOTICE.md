| material | why it is not here | how to obtain it |
|---|---|---|
| Competition task data: issue texts and repository snapshots and gold patches and tests and task metadata | owned by the competition organiser and redistribution is not allowed by the competition rules | from the competition's data page after accepting the competition rules. The per-task tables here carry task ids and outcome flags only |
| The public LoRA kit the training data came from (the code names it "Gluzdov LoRA-kit" with step records and `lora_index.csv`) and its trajectories and the training data built from them (`sft/data/*` and the history pack and the on-policy data bundles) | third-party data and data derived from it that reproduces its content | from the kit's public location named in the competition materials. `code/sft/build_sft.py` and the converters in `code/sft/` rebuild our training sequences from the kit's files. | The kit's own training and evaluation scripts and the kit archives (`gemma-4-serving-and-lora-verification-kit` and `gemma-4-task-soundness-and-local-results`) and the organiser's agent system prompt | third-party code and text | with the kit and the competition materials. The wrappers in `code/sft/` import the kit trainer by file path so training needs the kit next to them |
| Gemma chat template | text owned by Google. The Gemma terms of use apply and the author has not cleared redistribution | the file `chat_template.jinja` of the checkpoint `gemma-4-e4b-it-qat-w4a16-ct` (Gemma 4 E4B QAT with w4a16 compressed tensors; Kaggle Models `google/gemma-4` transformers variant). `code/templates/chat_template.matched.diff` holds only our edits. `code/templates/apply_template_diff.py ORIGINAL chat_template.matched.diff OUT` checks the sha256 of the original (`33204f1a...`) and of the result (`855bbbbb...`) |
| Gemma 4 E4B weights and the QAT checkpoint and our dequantized bf16 copy | model weights under the Gemma terms | the checkpoint from Kaggle Models. The dequantized copy is rebuilt from it with `code/tools/dequant_qat_v053.py` and its file hashes are in the contracts |
| Harness packages: `swegemma` (the project's conda environment name) and `adk_submission` and `adk_eval_core` and the serving stack recorded in the contracts (vLLM 0.19.1 and transformers 5.13.1 and Google ADK 1.36.1) | third-party software that the code in `code/` imports | with the competition materials (organiser packages) and from PyPI (vLLM and transformers and ADK) at the versions recorded in the contracts |
| LoRA adapters (M0 and M1 and Hq1 and A_onpol seeds 1 and 2 and T_sub and M1q seeds 1 and 2 and the others) | model weights trained on the kit data | identified by sha256 in `contracts/` and `preregistrations/` and not released. | Agent traces and patches and test outputs and model-server logs and sandbox memory-guard logs | they contain task text and repository content and are large | not released. | Internal working notes and advice and review notes and session notes and skeleton and manuscript drafts | not part of the public record | not released. The pre-registrations and the freeze receipts that fix the rules are included |

Task ids in the per-task tables (for example `rich_2725` and `mpmath__mpmath-854`) identify tasks of the competition and public repositories. The tables hold flags and counts only.
